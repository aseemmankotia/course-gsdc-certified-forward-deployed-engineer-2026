## Chapter 10: Client Discovery, Scoping, and Business Case Development — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Power/Interest grid: High power + High interest = ? | Manage Closely — most engagement effort, frequent detailed updates. Exam tip: this is the quadrant most likely to be tested with the 'wrong' distractor being 'Keep Satisfied'. |
| 2 | Power/Interest grid: High power + Low interest = ? | Keep Satisfied — periodic, high-level updates. Don't overload them with detail they don't want. |
| 3 | Power/Interest grid: Low power + High interest = ? | Keep Informed — regular, detailed updates since they're engaged but lack authority. |
| 4 | Power/Interest grid: Low power + Low interest = ? | Monitor — minimal effort, periodic awareness checks only. |
| 5 | MEDDIC stands for? | Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion. Used to qualify complex B2B deals with multiple stakeholders. |
| 6 | BANT stands for? | Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline. Simpler qualification framework than MEDDIC — lacks explicit champion/decision-process focus. |
| 7 | SPIN Selling question sequence? | Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff. Question-based discovery technique, not a deal-qualification checklist like BANT/MEDDIC. |
| 8 | ROI formula for business case? | ROI (%) = (Net Benefit / Cost) x 100. Exam tests calculating this from given cost and savings figures. |
| 9 | Payback period formula? | Payback Period = Total Cost / Periodic Savings. Example: $240,000 cost / $30,000 monthly savings = 8 months. |
| 10 | Executive summary structure rule for non-technical audiences? | Lead with business outcome and cost impact in first 1-2 sentences; subordinate technical detail. Exam tip: distractors often lead with architecture/technical depth — that's wrong for exec audiences. |
| 11 | How to present soft (non-quantifiable) benefits in a business case? | Include qualitatively, clearly labeled as supporting evidence — never convert to arbitrary dollar figures or merge with hard ROI numbers. |
| 12 | Standard scoping workshop agenda flow (2-hr format)? | 1) Business goals/success criteria, 2) Current-state pain points, 3) Future-state options & trade-offs, 4) Next steps/owners. Balances business and technical audiences. |
