## Full Exam Simulation & Test-Taking Strategy Cheat Sheet

### Time Budget
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total exam time | 90 minutes |
| Total questions | 40 |
| Raw average per question | ~2.25 min |
| First-pass target per question | ~1.5 min |
| Time used on first pass (40 × 1.5) | ~60 min |
| Buffer left for flagged review | ~30 min |

**Rule:** Never spend your full average time budget on the first pass — always leave a buffer.

### Flag-and-Return Tactics
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Fully confident | Answer, move on, do not flag |
| Down to 2 plausible options | Pick best guess, flag, move on |
| Totally unsure of all 4 | Eliminate what you can, guess, flag, move on |
| Never leave blank | A flagged guess beats a blank every time |

### Review Pass Order
1. Clear all flagged questions first (highest ROI).
2. If time remains, light scan of answered-but-not-flagged questions for misclicks.
3. Do NOT reread every question in order — wastes buffer time.
4. Only change an answer with a concrete new reason, not a hunch.

### Domain-by-Domain Review
- Overall score can mask one weak domain — always break down by domain after any simulation.
- Prioritize study time on the lowest-scoring domain(s), not a full re-do of everything.
- Retest that domain specifically before attempting a new full simulation.

### Recurring Trap Pattern (All Domains)
| Trap | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Partially-correct option | Satisfies some but not all stated constraints — always re-check against every requirement in the scenario |
| Deprecated/wrong-tool option | Sounds familiar but doesn't fit the specific scenario given |
| Overengineered option | Technically works but violates cost/simplicity constraints implied in the scenario |

**Rule:** Re-read the full scenario's constraints before finalizing — don't answer based on the first "correct-sounding" option.

### Complimentary Retake Strategy
- Treat your first attempt as your real, best-prepared attempt — not a throwaway.
- Complete full preparation and at least one full 40-question simulation before scheduling the real exam.
- Reserve the retake as a safety net for an unlucky miss, not a substitute for study.

### Simulation Purpose
- Builds timing endurance under real 90-minute pressure.
- Surfaces weak domains for targeted review.
- Does NOT predict exact real-exam content — focus on reasoning patterns, not memorized questions.
