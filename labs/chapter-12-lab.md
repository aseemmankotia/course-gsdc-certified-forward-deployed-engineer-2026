# Chapter 12 Lab: Full Exam Simulation and Test-Taking Strategy

**Estimated time:** 90 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a timed 40-question practice exam simulation under real 90-minute conditions, then review the score breakdown by domain and log which recurring trap patterns caused missed answers

## Key takeaway

Pace yourself at roughly 2 minutes per question, flag and return on uncertainty, and use the domain score breakdown to prepare for any retake.
