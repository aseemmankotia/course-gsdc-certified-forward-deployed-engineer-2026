# Chapter 9 Lab: Rapid Prototyping UI and Cloud Deployment

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Wrap the RAG pipeline from the previous chapter in a Streamlit app, containerize it with a Dockerfile, and outline a deployment path to a managed container service on any of AWS, Azure, or GCP with secrets pulled from an environment-based secrets manager

## Key takeaway

Prototype fast with Streamlit or Gradio, but ship like an engineer — containerized, secrets-managed, and observable.
